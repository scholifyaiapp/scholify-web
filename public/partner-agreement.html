Partner Programme Agreement

Version 1.0 · Effective 23 August 2026 · The current version is always available at scholifyapp.com/partner-agreement.html

Plain-English Summary

  • You earn 27% of the payments Scholify actually collects from learners you refer, inside an earning window of 1, 3 or 5 monthly payments per learner.
  • The window is locked when each learner first pays, based on your total unique paid learners at that moment: 1 payment to start, 3 payments from your 300th paid learner onward, 5 payments from your 600th. Earlier learners are not upgraded retroactively. Annual plans always earn once, on the full annual amount.
  • The window is a maximum, not a guarantee: commissions accrue only for payments actually collected. If a learner cancels, their window ends with their last paid month — we never pay for a month nobody paid us.
  • Your link uses 90-day first-touch attribution, made permanent when the learner registers. First valid partner wins.
  • Every commission passes a 30-day validation hold. Refunds and chargebacks void the related commission. Self-referrals never qualify.
  • Promote honestly, disclose that you are a partner, never spam, and never bid on Scholify's brand terms.
  • Either side can end the partnership at any time; validly earned, matured commissions are still paid out.
This summary is for convenience only. The full terms below are the agreement.

1.Parties and Acceptance

This Partner Programme Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between Scholify, the online ACCA study service operated at scholifyapp.com (referred to as "Scholify", "we", "us"), and the person or entity whose partner application is approved (the "Partner", "you").

You accept this Agreement by (a) ticking the acceptance box on the partner application, or (b) using your Partner Link or partner dashboard after approval — whichever happens first. If you do not agree, do not participate in the Programme.

3.Joining the Programme

  1. Application. Anyone may apply at scholifyapp.com/partners/apply. You must provide accurate information and be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live).
  2. Approval. Every application is reviewed individually. Approval, rejection and later revocation are at Scholify's reasonable discretion. Commissions accrue only while your partner status is active.
  3. One account. One partner account per person or entity. Duplicate or fake accounts may be removed and their commissions voided.

4.Attribution

  1. First touch, 90 days. When a visitor opens your Partner Link, your code is remembered on their device for 90 days. The first valid partner touch wins; a later partner's link does not displace it during that period.
  2. Permanent at registration. When that visitor creates a Scholify account within the 90-day window, the referral becomes permanently attributed to you.
  3. Technical basis. Attribution relies on the visitor's browser storage and our tracking systems operating normally. Purchases that cannot be technically attributed (for example, because the visitor cleared storage, used a different device or blocked storage) do not generate commissions. Scholify's records are decisive, applied in good faith.
  4. No self-referrals. Purchases made by you, through your own accounts, on your own devices, or for your own benefit do not qualify and are void.

5.Commissions

  1. Rate. You earn 27% of each Qualifying Payment made by a Referred Learner inside that learner's Earning Window. The rate applied is the one recorded on your partner account at the time the commission is created.
  2. Earning Window and performance tiers. Each Referred Learner's Earning Window is fixed at the moment of their first Qualifying Payment, based on how many Unique Paid Learners you have at that moment (counting that learner):
    TierYour Unique Paid LearnersEarning Window (monthly plans)
    Launch1 – 299First 1 Qualifying Payment
    Growth300 – 599First 3 Qualifying Payments
    Premier600 +First 5 Qualifying Payments
    Tiers are prospective only: reaching 300 or 600 does not change the window already locked on earlier Referred Learners. Your 300th and 600th paid learners themselves receive the higher window.
  3. Annual plans. A Referred Learner whose first Qualifying Payment is an annual subscription has an Earning Window of one payment at every tier — the full year is collected in that single payment, and you earn 27% of the full annual amount once. Annual renewals do not generate further commissions.
  4. Payment-by-payment accrual. The Earning Window is a maximum, not a guarantee. A commission is created only when a Qualifying Payment is actually collected. If a Referred Learner cancels, pauses or fails payment, their window simply ends with their last collected payment. No commission is owed on payments that never happened.
  5. Validation Hold. Each commission is pending for 30 days from creation. A refund or chargeback of the underlying payment voids the related commission at any time before it is paid out.
  6. Calculation. Commissions are calculated in the currency of the underlying payment (normally USD), on the amount actually collected, rounded to the nearest cent per commission. Your dashboard at scholifyapp.com/partners is the live record of clicks, referrals, commissions and their statuses.

6.Payouts

  1. When. Commissions that have passed the Validation Hold and remain valid become payable. Payouts are made periodically, and each payout is recorded against your account with a payout reference visible in your dashboard.
  2. How. You must provide accurate payout details when requested. Scholify may set a reasonable minimum payout amount and will state it in the dashboard or by email before it applies.
  3. Taxes. You are an independent contractor, not an employee, agent or representative of Scholify. You are solely responsible for declaring and paying any taxes or social charges on your commissions in your jurisdiction.
  4. Errors. Manifest errors (double-counting, technical duplication, commissions on voided payments) may be corrected by adjusting unpaid balances, with an explanation in your dashboard or by email.

7.Promotion Rules

The Programme pays for honest introductions, not for manufactured traffic. You agree that you will not:

  • make false or misleading claims about Scholify — including guarantees of exam success, guaranteed income from the Programme, or any suggestion that Scholify is affiliated with or endorsed by ACCA;
  • bid on or register "Scholify", "scholifyapp" or confusingly similar terms in paid search, domains, apps or social handles, or impersonate Scholify in any channel;
  • send spam (unsolicited bulk messages) in any channel, or post your link in ways that violate a platform's own rules;
  • use cookie stuffing, forced clicks, hidden iframes, toolbars, typosquatting or any mechanism that attributes visitors who did not knowingly click your link;
  • offer unauthorised rebates, cash-backs or side-payments to induce sign-ups, or misrepresent Scholify's prices or refund policy;
  • promote to or collect data from children, or use Scholify learner data for any purpose (you receive only aggregated statistics and never learner personal data).

Disclosure. Wherever you promote your Partner Link, you must clearly disclose that you may earn a commission (for example, "partner link" or "#ad"), as required by advertising-disclosure rules such as the US FTC Endorsement Guides and their local equivalents.

Violation of this Section is a material breach: Scholify may revoke your partner status and void commissions that have not yet been paid out, in addition to any other remedy.
